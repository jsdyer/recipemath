Sources, privacy & limitations

Canadian Nutrient File 2026

Loading source details…

The complete CNF 2026 release contains average values for 5,993 foods. Food descriptions distinguish raw, cooked, drained and other preparations. Choose the matching food; this tool does not transform a raw food into a cooked food.

Nutrients are expressed per 100 g of edible food. CNF 2026 portion conversion values are already grams. Refuse and cooking-yield records are not used as servings. One official record (food 1728, measure 1116) has no measure label and is omitted; that food remains usable by weight.

Health Canada: CNF documentation · Open Government Licence – Canada. Adapted from Health Canada data. This tool is not endorsed by Health Canada.

USDA FoodData Central

Optional USDA searches contact a protected service. Search terms and selected food IDs leave your browser; your recipe does not. Results retain their FDC identifier, data type, publication date and retrieval date. Branded products may differ from Canadian formulations. Missing nutrients remain unknown. Branded foods reported per 100 mL are not accepted without a verified weight conversion; millilitres are never assumed to equal grams. Per-serving label fields are never mixed with per-100-g nutrient amounts.

USDA FoodData Central · API guide and CC0 data terms.

Read the estimates carefully

Missing values are not zero. A “partial” value is a subtotal for ingredients with known data and lists what is missing. Serving sizes, ingredient choice, product formulation and preparation affect the result. Entering a finished dish weight accounts for its weight in the per-100-g calculation; it does not model cooking losses or nutrient retention. This is a planning tool, not medical advice or a regulatory nutrition label.

Private by choice

Recipe edits stay in memory until you choose Save on this device. Saved recipes contain a snapshot of each food's values so later data updates do not silently alter them. JSON imports are validated and do not execute code. The site may count page visits; it does not send recipe contents with those counts.
